FOUNDER JOURNAL

Rajib Hansda: The Vision Behind Storybook Studio

Turning Moments into Timeless Stories.

Every wedding is more than a celebration. It is a collection of emotions, traditions, promises, and unforgettable moments. Behind every beautiful photograph is a story waiting to be told. This belief is at the heart of Storybook Studio, a premium wedding photography and filmmaking brand founded by Rajib Hansda.

The Vision Behind Storybook Studio

Rajib Hansda is the Founder and Creative Director of Storybook Studio. His vision is to create wedding photographs and films that go beyond simply documenting an event. For him, every frame should preserve an emotion, every photograph should hold a memory, and every film should bring a beautiful moment back to life.

With a focus on creativity, elegance, and meaningful storytelling, he aims to give couples a visual experience that feels personal, timeless, and memorable.

More Than Wedding Photography

Storybook Studio is built around the idea that every couple has a unique story. From the joyful colours of Haldi and Mehendi to the emotional moments of the wedding ceremony, the brand focuses on capturing the details and emotions that make each celebration special.

Its creative approach brings together wedding photography, cinematic filmmaking, and thoughtful visual storytelling.

A Premium Approach to Every Story

  • Elegance: Creating refined and visually beautiful wedding memories.
  • Emotion: Capturing genuine moments and meaningful connections.
  • Timelessness: Producing photographs and films that couples can cherish for generations.
The goal is not simply to capture how a wedding looked, but to preserve how it felt.

Serving Couples Across India

Storybook Studio aims to bring its wedding photography and filmmaking services to couples across India, creating meaningful visual stories for celebrations of different cultures and traditions. Every project is approached with creativity, care, and attention to detail.

The Journey Continues

For Rajib Hansda, Storybook Studio represents more than a business. It is a creative vision built around preserving love, emotion, and memories through visual storytelling. As the brand continues to grow, its purpose remains the same: to turn meaningful moments into timeless stories.
